# Driver gallery sources

The current official F1 driver directory was read on 27 November 2026 UTC. Names, teams and nationality flags are factual 2026 directory fields. No proprietary headshot, number graphic, team pattern, logo or font was copied. A 2027 gallery is explicitly unavailable rather than borrowing this roster.

Wikipedia article pageimage mappings identified candidate portrait files; Commons imageinfo confirmed each file's creator, source and free licence. Retained metadata: commons-metadata.json. Twenty-two local portraits are credited individually at public/driver-portraits/CREDITS.md and in the UI. Source-reported years are not independently verified; clothing may show previous teams. Files are unmodified, with a CSS display crop.

Twenty-one portraits use Commons free licences. Lewis Hamilton's published government portrait is attributed to Lauren Hurley / No 10 Downing Street under OGL 3, with its required attribution statement. The direct National Archives licence fetch returned 403; the official indexed licence result confirmed commercial re-use with attribution, consistent with the Commons source licence. No access restriction was bypassed.

The gallery does not imply endorsement or determine private attributes from photographs. Displayed identity, team and flag come from the official directory; image association comes from the source article and file metadata. Existing F1DB standings remain a separate licensed source, not a roster or portrait feed.
