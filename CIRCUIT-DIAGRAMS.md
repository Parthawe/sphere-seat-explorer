# All-race 3D layout diagrams · 26 November 2026

Every listed 2026/2027 race edition has an interactive Three.js layout diagram: 47 editions, 27 distinct physical outlines. Existing seat studies stay separate and take priority in the coverage label when available. A diagram does not establish ticket inventory, bank locations, terrain, bridge decks, buildings, calibrated chairs or sightlines. These assets do not remove any `needs_assets` seat-model gate.

Source: https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits at commit `fda4a2f74728fa033c81ba8d3b628966a4a095d2`. Captured 26 November 2026. The repository publishes its vectors under MIT, © 2019–2025 Tomislav Bacinger; its full notice is retained in public/data/circuit-layouts-LICENSE.txt. The README acknowledges an initial Google My Maps circuit collection. No Google tiles, photography, Street View or proprietary 3D mesh is imported. These community outlines are neither official nor surveyed, and their racing-layout year is unverified. Historical configurations can differ from current layouts; keep that limit visible.

The selected WGS84 coordinate sequences are centered in local meter space with north as negative Z and rounded to two decimals. No altitude field enters the viewer: the repository describes some altitudes as city-level references, which cannot supply road elevation. Plan paths close explicitly and draw as display ribbons on a tabletop. The rendered depth is not physical terrain. Suzuka's crossing remains a plan-diagram intersection, not a fabricated bridge model. Austria and other elevated circuits remain without terrain or chair cameras here.

The event-to-physical-outline index explicitly maps the 2026 Bahrain GP in Malaysia to Sepang (`my-1999`), while 2027 Bahrain uses Sakhir (`bh-2002`). Madrid and Barcelona, Portimão and other Portuguese circuits, and each other identity remain distinct. The source-year boundary applies to both 2026 and 2027 diagram entry; a later calendar date does not verify the outline's configuration.

Navigation: each race guide exposes 3D circuit diagram; the viewer has race/season selection, orbit, overhead, bounded zoom, keyboard +/−/Home, reset and optional animated marker. The marker follows source vertex order at a constant illustrative lap period, not historical direction, speed or timing. Camera transitions respect reduced motion. Static scenes render on demand, hidden tabs suspend frames, and data/WebGL failures offer Retry and the race guide. Controls are measured outside the canvas region to prevent phone overlap.

Remaining work: licensed current edition/category/stand/vertical data is still required to expand actual seat studies. Follow MAPPING-PROGRESS.json for that programme; do not relabel these diagrams as completed venue seating reconstructions.
