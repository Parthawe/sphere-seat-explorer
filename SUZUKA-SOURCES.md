# Suzuka 2027 source audit

Checked 25 November 2026. [F1’s event](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2027/japan) and [the circuit’s English event landing page](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/eng/f1/index.html?direct=true) confirm 9–11 April 2027 at Suzuka. The [Japanese ticket catalogue](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/f1/ticket/index.html) explicitly covers 2027. Some English ticket roots still identify 2026, so they are not a basis for new season layout, pricing, sale or access claims. No prices or live stock are copied.

The current catalogue supplies nineteen mapped grandstand families: V1,V2,A1,A2,B1,B2,C,D,E,G,H,I,M,O,P,Q1,Q2,R,S. Product links in src/suzuka.ts are taken from this catalogue. Q1-1/Q1-2 have different seating types. V1/V2 are different levels; outlet variants need separate visibility checks. S/S-BOX share a source page but do not become one surveyed box layout. Broad G/D family anchors do not represent all of their subsections. Eleven separate handoffs cover West Area,wheelchair tickets,photographer areas,Ferris Wheel Lounge,VIP Suite Premium,R-BOX,Paddock Club,organizer-linked Champions Club,Thursday-only,Thursday–Friday and the age-limited ZERO programme. There is no certified route, room/table, platform or roaming view.

The catalogue lists MobilityStation and directs some countries/regions to F1 Tickets. The app links the original catalogue and organizer-linked seller, without entering queues or authenticated selection/checkout. The website’s robots.txt returned 404 during the bounded public catalogue read; no access restriction was bypassed. The source HTML and official illustrative map were inspected locally, not redistributed. Their Google/DigitalGlobe imagery credits do not grant an imagery licence to this app.

It retains a local meter trace as well as a north-up pixel projection and 29 tagged grandstand envelopes. src/suzuka-track.json and public/data/suzuka-track.json are identical ODbL adaptations with provenance and downloadable metadata. The full temporary XML snapshot is not published.

No historical Suzuka telemetry or dated attendee review collection was added.

## Start/finish audit and vertical-context checkpoint

The current Japanese product pages link six original schematic charts: [V1](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/f1/ticket/images/seat/pdf/f1_v1.pdf), [V2](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/f1/ticket/images/seat/pdf/f1_v2.pdf), [A1](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/f1/ticket/images/seat/pdf/f1_a1.pdf), [A2](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/f1/ticket/images/seat/pdf/f1_a2.pdf), [B1](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/f1/ticket/images/seat/pdf/f1_b1.pdf), [B2](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/f1/ticket/images/seat/pdf/f1_b2.pdf). All label themselves schematic. V1 page 1 was visually inspected; block/row/number labels and a tier overview establish label references, not measured elevations or georeferenced chair coordinates. V1/V2 categories and restricted-view variants remain distinct; B1’s original examples identify obstructions that differ by position and direction. Six direct source links now sit beside the applicable area selection. No chart or photograph is republished.

research/suzuka/start-finish-envelope-audit.json retains horizontal minimum-area fits for named V1/V2/A1/B1/B2 ODbL envelopes. Their unequal, curved or shared shapes do not establish uniform event chair grids. A2 still lacks a named source-envelope association. No new source bbox acquisition was needed.

The [GSI elevation API documentation](https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/elevation_s.html) and [current GSI content terms](https://www.gsi.go.jp/kikakuchousei/kikakuchousei40182.html) were inspected. Exactly three documented API queries, retained separately from OSM in research/suzuka/elevation-diagnostic.json, returned elevation/source pairs: crossover plan-center 43.4 m, V1-envelope context 36.4 m and Spoon-road context 57.6 m; each identifies 5m laser DEM as its source. These are three ground-context samples, not a 5m-resolution reconstructed terrain field, seat-base elevations or a road-deck survey. One DEM value at a horizontal crossing cannot recover both decks or measured clearance. No value enters the runtime map, bridge, eye metrics or other scenes. The artifact retains source/terms URLs; future processing must preserve GSI attribution/processing notices separately from ODbL.

The vertical-context gap remains. Keep the functioning area map, original view references and source limits; do not render a false flat figure-eight. The next independent venue is Monaco. Return to Suzuka 3D only with new supporting terrain/structural evidence rather than repeating the same diagnostic requests.
