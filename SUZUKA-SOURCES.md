# Suzuka 2027 source audit

Checked 25 November 2026. [F1’s event](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2027/japan) and [the circuit’s English event landing page](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/eng/f1/index.html?direct=true) confirm 9–11 April 2027 at Suzuka. The [Japanese ticket catalogue](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/f1/ticket/index.html) explicitly covers 2027. Some English ticket roots still identify 2026, so they are not a basis for new season layout, pricing, sale or access claims. No prices or live stock are copied.

The current catalogue supplies nineteen mapped grandstand families: V1,V2,A1,A2,B1,B2,C,D,E,G,H,I,M,O,P,Q1,Q2,R,S. Product links in src/suzuka.ts are taken from this catalogue. Q1-1/Q1-2 have different seating types. V1/V2 are different levels; outlet variants need separate visibility checks. S/S-BOX share a source page but do not become one surveyed box layout. Broad G/D family anchors do not represent all of their subsections. Eleven separate handoffs cover West Area,wheelchair tickets,photographer areas,Ferris Wheel Lounge,VIP Suite Premium,R-BOX,Paddock Club,organizer-linked Champions Club,Thursday-only,Thursday–Friday and the age-limited ZERO programme. There is no certified route, room/table, platform or roaming view.

The catalogue lists MobilityStation and directs some countries/regions to F1 Tickets. The app links the original catalogue and organizer-linked seller, without entering queues or authenticated selection/checkout. The website’s robots.txt returned 404 during the bounded public catalogue read; no access restriction was bypassed. The source HTML and official illustrative map were inspected locally, not redistributed. Their Google/DigitalGlobe imagery credits do not grant an imagery licence to this app.

One public OSM API bbox snapshot (136.515,34.835,136.552,34.859) contains [circuit relation 284570](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/284570). scripts/prepare-suzuka-map.py orders its forty GP ways into 269 closed vertices, approximately 5,813 m against F1’s stated 5.807 km, excluding pit lane 120917578 and unrelated karting/South/West variants. It retains a local meter trace as well as a north-up pixel projection and 29 tagged grandstand envelopes. src/suzuka-track.json and public/data/suzuka-track.json are identical ODbL adaptations with provenance and downloadable metadata. The full temporary XML snapshot is not published.

Named source anchors include V1(184107052),V2(183394522),A1(184120096),B1(184143131),B2(184143132),C(184143133),D family(469368057/469395390),E(467982372),G family(184102012 / 184102013 / 184102361 / 184102368),M(183953761),O(184415310),P(184419745),Q1(183393132),Q2(183393101/102/103),R(183393129),S(183394069). A2,H,I markers are authored broad corner regions from the organizer map and GP geography. No anonymous envelope is identified as an official product. Named N/L source envelopes are not silently promoted to new reserved 2027 products; western roaming remains source-only.

Bridge way 175231434 has bridge=yes/layer=1 and crosses covered GP 183391655. The plan map redraws the upper segment over the lower trace. These tags establish order, not clearance, supports, ground elevations or stand tier heights. There is no 3D scene or false flat crossover. Further representative 3D needs an explicit supported treatment of the vertical route and venue context; do not reacquire the same GP geography expecting missing heights to appear.

The [V1/V2 and pit-building diagram](https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/f1/ticket/pdf/grandstand_f1.pdf) is an external original reference. Product pages expose original seating/view controls, including 360° and YouTube links where supplied. No capture year, exact-ticket calibration or current playback is certified. No historical Suzuka telemetry or dated attendee review collection was added.
