## Application pack

Supply actual operator name/contact/country; f1tickets.online and a short explanation of the independent pre-purchase comparison experience; real audience figures if available, otherwise say pre-launch/unmeasured; reference screenshots and source/accuracy policy; the permission document and its applicable scope as needed; intended event and categories. Do not claim traffic, conversions, official affiliation or blanket seat accuracy.

Ask for the exact agreement terms above, permitted attribution test method, whether noreferrer is compatible, reporting access/export, refund/chargeback treatment, tax/payment onboarding and permission for any supplied assets. Do not adopt a hosted replacement site or change DNS merely to join a programme.

After acceptance the partner issues a tracking URL or code tied to our account. A public catalogue URL or signup page cannot be converted into our affiliate link by guessing an ID or adding query parameters. Keep private agreements and account credentials outside the repository.
