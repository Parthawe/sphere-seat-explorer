## Race discovery and guide flow · v124

The next human request was to improve the whole experience. This pass concentrates on choosing a race and finding its viewing information. On the 390px Qatar baseline, the stand catalogue began at roughly document y=2694 after circuit facts and schedule; the final layout places it at y=876. This is a layout measurement, not a speed benchmark.

- Put stands and viewing tools ahead of schedule/circuit facts. Separated references from the catalogue in the guide while preserving VenueStands' default behavior elsewhere.
- Consolidated the repeated breadcrumb into a race selector with round labels and an All races return. Changing seasons keeps the selected race when it exists in the destination calendar; the destination edition still resolves its own model/outline.
- Added a measured sticky section bar, scroll offsets and current-section semantics. Its height updates only when it changes; scroll work is queued into one frame. All six links retain keyboard focus and visible headings.
- Replaced the catalogue's nested scroll region with six initial options and an explicit Show all action. Search and filters reach the complete catalogue. New stand anchors clear incompatible filters and reveal all options; collapsing afterward remains possible. An early harness found this filtered-anchor case and it was fixed before publication.
- Qatar/Yas map-and-locator desks now mount only when requested. Once opened, they remain mounted while collapsed to retain entered text. Links can reopen the desk even at the same hash. Initial hidden desk mounting was found to populate an area query and could open tools on a later render; lazy mounting prevents that side effect. Area-only venues keep their primary maps visible.
- Replaced the redundant all-circuit-studies filter with Circuit outlines, separate from Seat studies. Kept search text at 16px on narrow layouts.
