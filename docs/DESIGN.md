
### Lighter typography · 26 November 2026

Use regular 400 for body text and routine controls, with medium 500 for headings, names and emphasis. The previous 600/700 interface weights are retired. Retain the existing Barlow Condensed / IBM Plex Sans families and the red/ink palette. Do not use decorative eyebrow text above headings or small brand subtitles. Preserve unique dates, race identity and historical/source context as ordinary readable text. Section hierarchy comes from size, space and grouping. Medium italic is loaded for existing explorer headings; no synthetic bold is needed.

### Calendar opening hierarchy · 26 November 2026

The calendar entrance uses one task row: a medium-weight primary headline with a smaller supporting line, paired with a native race selector and a single “Explore race” submit action. Selection opens that exact event/season's guide. A wide Las Vegas example scene follows, with metadata and credits below the visual rather than obscuring it. The header owns navigation and season switching; narrow screens place navigation on a second header row and stack the introduction, selector and scene in DOM order. “Race weekends” introduces the chronological highlights; “All [year] races” introduces the searchable full calendar. Keep the established 400/500 typography, no decorative eyebrows and explicit illustrative/source limitations.
