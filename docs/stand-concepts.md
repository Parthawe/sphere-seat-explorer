# Historical generic 3D concepts · disabled

Current state, checked 27 November 2026: these generic concept scenes were removed from product navigation in v104 after the user requested actual venue positions. Legacy links now recover to stand evidence. The section below records the former implementation; it does not describe current seating coverage. Reintroducing illustrative simulations requires a clear product choice and explicit distinction from venue-based studies.

## Former implementation · 26 November 2026

The owner explicitly requested illustrative 3D for the catalogue options. The new stand-study layer provides authored, isolated concept scenes for 183 known viewing options that do not have an existing venue model. It does not change their source confidence or promote missing venue/terrain assets to completed reconstruction.

Five formats are supported: generic grandstand, open admission, hospitality deck, viewing platform and a multiple-stand example. Grandstands offer orbit and a sample seated camera with 16 example rows/24 positions. Those numbers and every dimension, tier, facing, boundary and straight road segment are invented for the illustration. There is no geographic placement, actual circuit outline, current stand envelope, surveyed elevation, building, canopy, accessible route or calibrated seat view in these scenes. The label “Concept illustration” and the statement that this is not a preview of the location remain visible before the scene and controls.

The classifier is deliberately separate from catalogue evidence. It selects a generic illustration format from retained product names/types; it does not validate a product's actual architecture. The same authored format can serve multiple entries. A platform concept does not certify accessible dimensions, companion seating or suitability; a lounge concept does not identify an actual suite/table/package view. Three sample stands do not invent a hopping package's day allocation. Original source notes remain available beside the scene. No concept is a buying recommendation or inventory claim.

Existing venue-model links (148 entries across current catalogue editions, including dated Vegas references) stay intact. The new layer adds 115 grandstand, 35 hospitality, 12 open-admission, 11 platform and 10 multi-stand concepts. Seven non-viewing service/reference/eligibility entries remain source-only: no fake stand is created for camping/transport, tours or programmes. Empty/unverified race-edition catalogues do not acquire invented stand names.

Navigation: catalogue rows retain their original model/area/source destination and gain a separate Open 3D concept action when appropriate. A 3D concepts filter isolates the new examples. Routes bind season, race and known option ID; invalid or cross-edition options do not render a canvas. Non-grandstand formats do not expose sample-seat inputs. Back returns to the venue catalogue. Shared links identify the option, not a ticket or actual chair.

Rendering uses instanced authored seating, materials, lighting and shadows without new dependencies, remote imagery or venue meshes. Cameras ease between orbit and sample views; reduced motion settles immediately and static scenes render on demand. Hidden tabs stop frames. A local graphics boundary/context-loss handler supports Retry. No live timing or moving race data is implied.

This release expands illustration coverage, not verified stand or seat coverage. Actual per-venue studies still require suitable physical and vertical evidence. Existing source-only, original-year, commercial, privacy and live-feed limits remain unchanged.
